## Introduction

Kal yaani 12 September 2026 ko Delhi ka Bharat Mandapam ek aisi meeting ka gawah banega jo shayad agle kaafi saalon tak AI ki duniya ko naya shape de.

18th BRICS Summit — India ki chairmanship mein — New Delhi mein ho raha hai 12 aur 13 September 2026 ko. Aur is baar ki baat sirf trade deals ya diplomatic bayan tak nahi ruki. Is baar AI ek central agenda item hai.

11 member countries, duniya ki leading emerging economies, ek jagah baith ke ye decide karenge ki artificial intelligence ko Global South ke liye kaise use kiya jaaye — aur kaun is game ko lead karega.

India ne apni chairmanship ka theme rakha hai "Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability" yaani BRICS — jis naam se ye group jaana jaata hai wahi unka theme bhi hai. Aur Innovation mein AI sabse aage hai.

Toh exactly kya hone wala hai? India ka kya plan hai? Aur aam insaan ke liye iska kya matlab hai?



## BRICS Hai Kya — Pehle Thoda Background

BRICS originally 5 countries ka group tha — Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa. Ye naam inhi ke pehle alphabets se bana tha.

2024 mein ye group expand hua aur ab 11 members hain — Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, Saudi Arabia, UAE, aur Argentina bhi is mein aa gaye hain. Ye sab milke duniya ki 45% population represent karte hain aur global GDP ka roughly 35% inke paas hai.

Matlab jab BRICS kuch decide karta hai toh wo sirf kuch deshon ki baat nahi hoti — ye duniya ke ek bahut bade hisse ki baat hoti hai.



## AI BRICS Agenda Mein Kaise Aaya?

2026 mein India ne BRICS ki chairmanship sambhali aur pehle din se hi technology aur AI ko priority list mein rakha.

India ka argument seedha aur strong hai — Western countries AI mein pehle se bahut aage hain. US, UK, EU ke paas bade AI companies hain, funding hai, aur talent bhi hai. Global South ke desh agar abhi nahi jaage toh ye AI divide aur bada ho jaayega — aur developing countries technology ke consumers ban jayenge, creators nahi.

Isliye India chahta hai ki BRICS countries milke apna AI ecosystem banayein — joint research karein, data share karein, aur ek doosre ke digital infrastructure pe kaam karein.

February 2026 mein jab India ne AI Impact Summit host ki — jo Global South ka pehla kabhi AI summit tha — tab bhi yahi message tha. Ab BRICS Summit mein India us message ko aur aage le jaana chahta hai.



## BRICS Summit 2026 Mein AI Ke Liye Kya Hone Wala Hai?

Agenda mein kai specific cheezein hain jo AI se directly connected hain.

Pehli baat hai AI governance cooperation. BRICS desh milke ek shared framework banana chahte hain — koi common rules jo ye decide karein ki AI kaise develop ho, kaise use ho, aur kaise regulate ho. Abhi har desh apne hisaab se chal raha hai. Ek shared BRICS approach se Global South ki ek collective awaaz ban sakti hai global AI policy mein.

Doosri baat hai shared AI research aur startup ecosystem. India ek startup innovation fund propose karne wala hai aur ek incubator network bhi — jahan BRICS ke students, researchers, aur entrepreneurs milke AI projects par kaam kar sakein. Ek shared research repository bhi propose hai jisme BRICS countries apna data aur findings ek doosre ke saath share karein.

Teesri aur bahut important baat hai digital public infrastructure. India ka UPI aur Aadhaar duniya mein already successful models hain. Ab India chahta hai ki in models ko BRICS countries mein export kiya jaaye — aur AI se aur better banaya jaaye. Digital identity, digital payments, aur AI-powered government services — ye sab ek package mein Global South ke liye available ho sakti hain.

Chauthi baat hai AI aur semiconductors par deeper cooperation. Chips AI ka backbone hain — bina semiconductors ke koi bhi AI model nahi chal sakta. BRICS countries milke semiconductor supply chain mein ek doosre par depend karna chahte hain — US aur Taiwan pe poori dependency khatam karna chahte hain.



## India Ko Kya Milega Is Summit Se?

India ke liye BRICS Summit sirf ek diplomatic photo op nahi hai — iske kuch concrete faayde hain.

Sabse pehla fayda hai global AI leadership ki image banana. India ne February mein AI Impact Summit host ki, ab September mein BRICS mein AI agenda lead kar raha hai. Ye consistently ek message send kar raha hai ki AI governance mein India ek serious player hai — sirf Silicon Valley ya Brussels nahi, Delhi bhi matters karta hai.

Doosra fayda hai market access. BRICS mein ab 11 countries hain. Agar India apna AI model — aur saath mein UPI, Aadhaar jaise digital tools — in countries mein export kar sake toh ye Indian tech companies ke liye ek bahut bada market ban sakta hai.

Teesra fayda hai technology ke mamle mein diversification. Abhi India kaafi AI technology America aur China se import karta hai. Agar BRICS framework mein joint AI research aur development hogi toh India ki dependency thodi reduce hogi.

Chautha fayda hai Global South mein leadership. 11 member countries mein se zyada tar developing nations hain. Agar India inhe AI mein guide kare toh ye ek soft power ka bahut bada source banta hai — China ke alternative ke roop mein India ki position mazboot hogi.



## Kya Challenges Hain?

Itni achi planning ke bawajood kuch real hurdles bhi hain.

BRICS mein China bhi hai — aur China ka apna bahut bada AI ecosystem hai Baidu, Alibaba, Huawei ke saath. India aur China ki rivalry toh jaani pehchani hai. Toh jab dono ek hi table par baith ke AI cooperation karne ki baat karenge toh actually kitna kuch ho payega — ye dekhna hoga.

Russia bhi member hai — aur Russia ke saath bahut saare Western countries ke sanctions hain. Iska matlab hai ki kuch global AI tools aur partnerships complicated ho jaati hain BRICS framework ke andar.

Aur ek practical challenge hai data sovereignty. Agar BRICS countries data share karein AI training ke liye toh har desh apna data doosre ke server pe jaane dene mein comfortable nahi hoga — ye politically aur security-wise sensitive issue hai.



## Aam Insaan Ke Liye Iska Kya Matlab Hai?

Ye sab diplomatic baat sunke lagta hai ki aam zindagi se kya lena dena — par actually connection hai.

Agar BRICS AI cooperation successful rahi toh India mein AI tools aur services sasti aur accessible hogi — kyunki India apni technology khud develop kar raha hoga, sirf import nahi kar raha.

Sarkari services AI se better hogi — healthcare, education, agriculture mein real world impact aayega.

Indian tech companies ke liye 11 BRICS deshon mein market opportunity milegi — matlab zyada jobs, zyada opportunities.

Aur AI governance mein agar India ki awaaz strong rahi toh AI ke rules wo insaan ke hisaab se banen — sirf bade tech companies ke profit ke hisaab se nahi.



## Conclusion

BRICS Summit 2026 ek aisi moment hai jahan India AI ki global chess board par ek bada move karne ki koshish kar raha hai.

Sapne bade hain — shared AI governance, joint research, digital infrastructure export, semiconductor cooperation. Aur honestly, challenges bhi bade hain — China ke saath competition, Russia ki complication, aur data sovereignty ke sawaal.

Par ek baat clear hai. AI sirf ek technology nahi raha — ye ek geopolitical power ka naya zariya ban gaya hai. Aur India is power game mein apni jagah banana chahta hai — sirf observer ki taraf se nahi, balki ek active player ki taraf se.

Aane wale 48 ghante — 12 aur 13 September — ye batayenge ki BRICS mein AI cooperation sirf kaagaz par rahegi ya actually kuch concrete outcomes aayenge.

Aapko kya lagta hai — BRICS AI agenda se India ko faayda hoga ya ye sirf ek diplomatic exercise hai? Neeche comment mein zaroor batao.
