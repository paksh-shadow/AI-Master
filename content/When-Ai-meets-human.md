# AI Kehti Hai Haan, Dil Kehta Hai Ruko: Faisla Kiska Chalega?

Pichle kuch mahino mein maine khud dekha hai ki log AI se kya-kya puchne lage hain. Email likhwana toh purani baat ho gayi. Ab log career badalne, business band karne, kisi ko job se nikalne tak ki salah AI se le rahe hain.

Mujhe isme ek cheez khatakti hai. AI ka jawab hamesha itna *confident* hota hai ki shak karne ka mann hi nahi karta.

[Yahan apna ek chhota sa experience likhiye: kab AI ne aapko kuch bataya jo sunne mein sahi tha, par nikla galat, ya jahan aapne uski baat na maan kar sahi kiya.]

## Data poori kahani nahi batata

Ek example lijiye. Aapki dukaan hai aur aap AI ko pichle saal ki sales, season ka trend, sab de dete hain. AI kehta hai: agle mahine fulan item ka stock badha do.

Theek hai. Par aapko pata hai ki agle mahine aapki gali mein sadak khudne wali hai. Customer aayega hi nahi. AI ko ye pata nahi tha, kyunki kisi spreadsheet mein ye likha hi nahi tha.

AI galat nahi tha. Usse jo mila, usne uske hisaab se sahi kiya. Dikkat ye hai ki jo cheez dikhti nahi, wo bhi decision ka hissa hoti hai.

Yahi baat office mein bhi lagu hoti hai. Dashboard kehta hai ki ek employee ka output gir raha hai. Par manager ko pata hai ki wo bandha do logon ki problems suljha raha hai, aur uska wo kaam kisi number mein nahi dikhta. Ya uske ghar mein koi mushkil chal rahi hai. Sirf number dekh kar usse "low performer" bol dena aasan hai. Sahi nahi.

Ab ulta bhi sach hai: insaan bhi kam galat nahi karte. Manager ko apna favourite pasand hota hai, hum sab ke biases hote hain. Isliye mera maanna ye hai ki AI aur insaan ek-doosre ke vikalp nahi hain. Ek ke paas numbers hain, doosre ke paas context. Dono chahiye.

## Sabse bada khatra: hum sochna band kar dete hain

AI ki galti utni badi baat nahi hai. Badi baat ye hai ki hum uski baat bina jaanche maan lete hain.

Isko automation bias kehte hain. Jab koi machine sunder tareeke se, logic ke saath jawab de, toh hum apna dimaag lagana kam kar dete hain. Socho, recruiter ko AI ne bola ki ye candidate fit nahi hai. Resume dekhe bina reject. Ho sakta hai us bande ka career path alag tha, aur AI ne usse "negative signal" maan liya. Ek achha candidate hath se gaya, aur kisi ko pata bhi nahi chala.

Isliye mera ek simple rule hai: AI ka jawab *shuruaat* hai, faisla nahi. Aur jitna bada daanv, utni zyada jaanch. Naukri, paisa, sehat, kanoon ke maamlon mein toh aur bhi.

## Kahan insaan ki samajh ka koi vikalp nahi

Bhavnayein. AI sahanubhuti wale shabd likh sakta hai. Par jab koi mushkil mein hota hai, toh use sirf sahi shabd nahi chahiye hote. Use woh insaan chahiye jo samjhe. Ye farq mehsoos hota hai.

Naitikta. Maan lo ek company kharcha kam karne ke liye logon ko nikalna chahti hai. AI bata dega ki kitna paisa bachega. Par us paise ke peeche kisi ke ghar ka chulha bhi hai. Ye hisaab kisi formula mein nahi aata. Company ko khud tay karna padega ki wo kya log hain.

Anokhi situations. AI ne jo pattern dekha hai, wahi wo pehchanta hai. Kabhi aisa waqt aata hai jab sab kuch "normal" dikhta hai par kuch theek nahi lagta. Experienced insaan ko wo ahsaas hota hai. Use sunna chahiye.

Zimmedari. AI report bana sakta hai, action automate kar sakta hai. Par agar kuch galat ho gaya, toh jawab kaun dega? Aap. Aapki company. Machine ko koi court nahi bulayega.

## Toh AI ke saath kaam kaise karein?

Mera tareeka kuch aisa hai:

1. Pehle apni samajh banao.** Kisi bade decision mein AI se puchne se pehle khud soch lo ki aap kya karenge. Phir AI se compare karo. Aise aap uske jawab se anchor nahi hote.
2. Sirf jawab mat maango, uske peeche ki sochh bhi maango. "Is salah mein kaunse assumptions hain? Kya cheez missing ho sakti hai? Agar main ulta karun toh kya hoga?" Ye teen sawal kaafi hain.
3. Apna context jodo. Aap apne customer, apni team, apne shehar ko jaante hain. AI nahi jaanta.
4. Faisla aapka, zimmedari bhi aapki.
5. Baad mein dekho kya hua. AI sahi tha ya aapki samajh? Is se agli baar ke liye seekh milti hai.

## Kya hum soch-ne ki aadat kho rahe hain?

Ye sawal mujhe thoda bechain karta hai. Likhne ke liye AI. Research ke liye AI. Plan ke liye AI. Ab faisle ke liye bhi AI.

Agar isse aapka kaam behtar ho raha hai, toh bilkul theek. Par ek test hai: kya aap AI ke bina bhi soch sakte hain? Agar jawab "mushkil lagta hai" hai, toh madad nahi, nirbharta ho gayi hai.

Aur AI se asehmat hona seekhiye. Agar uska jawab aapke tajurbe se mel nahi khata, toh turant apna tajurba mat phenk dijiye. Dono ko tolna sikhiye.

Yahan likhiye aapne khud kis cheez mein AI par bharosa kam kiya, ya kab AI se asehmat hue.

## Aage kya?

Mujhe nahi lagta ki insaan ki samajh ki keemat kam hone wali hai. Routine kaam toh AI sambhal lega, aur sambhalna bhi chahiye. Par tab jo sawal bachenge wo zyada mushkil honge: Hum asal mein kya hal karna chahte hain? AI ko kya lakshya dein? Kya ye faisla sahi hai, sirf sasta nahi? Aur kab AI ko "nahi" bolna hai?

Aane wale waqt mein faida unhein hoga jo AI chalana jaante hain *aur* apni sochne ki taqat bhi bachaye rakhte hain.

## Aakhir mein

Toh AI par bharosa karein ya shak? Dono. Jaise kisi achhe doctor ki baat sunne ke baad bhi bade operation se pehle doosri raay lete hain, waise hi.

Sawal ye nahi ki machine kitni smart ho gayi. Sawal ye hai ki hum *tez* faisle chahte hain, ya *behtar* faisle?
